## What was integrated (branch `asr-cuda-perf`, from main 69ac70d)

- `asr-host-profile` (`--profile-host`, `--pass-lanes`, fd limit / accept
  back-off, GPU bench tools) and `asr-bf16tc` (`--precision bf16 --gemm own-tc`)
  were already merged.
- `asr-stage-graphs` merged (split step `submit`/`finish`, `--stage-ahead`,
  `--host-threads`, `--graphs buckets`, `--graph-buckets`, `--warmup`, gate C).
  - Engine host phases re-placed: `cuda_step_submit` charges `reset`,
    `pass_build` (lane choice + staging every pass), `enqueue` (H2D + encoder
    of pass 0); `cuda_step_finish` charges `enqueue` for later passes and
    `pass_decode` charges `dec_launch`, `enc_wait`, `dec_wait`, `final_wait`,
    `detok`. The untimed remainder of submit + finish goes to `pass_build`, so
    the eight phases sum to the step wall (submit + finish).
  - Server loop: new phase `window` (the stage-ahead scan between submit and
    finish, and the pre-scan at the top of the loop); `step` no longer contains
    it.
  - own-tc under graphs: the launcher allocates nothing, never synchronises,
    queries no device attribute (splits = f(N, K)) and its workspace is sized at
    open for every padded bucket shape, so it is capture-safe as is. Gate C
    with bf16 own-tc: 85/85 passes from graphs, byte-identical.
- `asr-cuda-quant` merged (`--kv-dtype f32|bf16|int8`, `--weights f32|int8`,
  `gemm_w8.cu`). `--weights int8` with `--gemm own-tc` is refused (one GEMM per
  weight); the resolved precision reads `bf16|f32|w8a32` + K/V suffix. The K/V
  arena is sized over `arena_slots`, so the graph scratch slot has a ring in
  every K/V dtype.
- Tooling: `gpu_knee.sh` takes `ONSETS` (speech-onset TTFP); `gpu_qualify.sh`
  takes `--server-args` (recorded in manifest.json).
- Mac CPU build, `check_flag_registry.py`, `check_plan.py`,
  `check_repo_integrity.py`: PASS.

## Reading

- On the L40S the f32 arm is device-bound, not host-bound: a step is ~72 ms of
  which ~70 ms is `finish` (the engine thread waiting on the encoder + label
  loop), at 59 % SM busy and with software power capping 43 % of the time.
  SM % (a sampled kernel-resident fraction) understates how long the f32
  pass occupies the device; the step wall is the measure.
- bf16 own-tc cuts the step to ~12.5 ms (-83 %) and takes the lag p95 from
  256 to 53 ms at C384; it is the change that moves the knee.
- The serving loop (C) takes the host enqueue off the step (submit 1.8 ->
  0.1 ms, every pass from a graph) but at these loads the step is already a
  small fraction of the 40 ms cohort cadence, so B and C are
  indistinguishable on latency at C384 (53 vs 53 ms lag p95; the TTFP penalty
  +12 vs +42 ms is within one rung's noise and not yet attributable).
- What limits next: neither device nor engine thread yet. At C512 the step is
  ~14 ms per 40 ms cadence and SM busy 35 %; the next limits are the cohort
  cadence (step wall grows ~0.6 ms per +64 streams) and the 30.7-CPU cgroup
  shared by server (host mel ~100 s of CPU per 150 s rung at C512, spread over
  the 4-thread feed team) and the load generator.
