# IT-120M accuracy frontier: can the streaming specialist reach stable < 20 WER? (roadmap, 2026-10-12)

Research question:

> Can the 120M streaming specialist reach stable < 20 macro WER, while preserving its serving/EOU
> advantages, through better training exposure and teacher distillation rather than increased model size?

Milestones: < 30 stable, then < 25, then < 20. A ~10 target is investigated only if the scaling
curve supports it. Nothing here assumes 3-8 WER is reachable by a 120M model.

Product hypothesis (the reason to keep the 120M): a small per-language streaming specialist with a
true <EOU>, low VRAM, high concurrency, low latency and cheap specialisation. ~15-20 stable WER at
far better economics than a 0.6B / large ASR is already a strong result. Every accuracy milestone
should eventually carry: model size / VRAM, streaming latency, concurrency, beam CPU cost, EOU
behaviour, and $/stream once benchmarks exist.

Accuracy research and EOU are separate: plain-ASR frontier first; stage 2 (EOU) on the plain winner
afterwards. The EOU-v3 chosen on 2026-10-11 stays the deployable checkpoint; EOU is NOT retrained
after every plain ablation.

Other facts to carry:
- The ~49 wall was the recipe: the encoder barely adapted under the freeze curriculum (E1a decoder/joint

- C1 with the cosine over 12k steps was still improving at 9k (41.15) when it died of OOM: the old
  4k budget/schedule undertrained. No 12k endpoint exists.
- E3G shows only that THIS replacement mix (Granary takes 50 % of draws, halving A2 exposure) at THIS
  4k budget did not move E3's macro (MLS +2.6, VP -1.4, CV/FLEURS within noise). It does NOT show that
  Granary is useless as a reservoir.
- Eval noise floor ~0.5 macro / ~0.8 per domain (A2 vs B2 before their recipes diverge). One seed.
- Gap decomposition on E3 (harness above): greedy -> beam 3.4, beam -> oracle 4.6, oracle -> teacher
  12.8. The oracle -> teacher gap is a candidate/model/evidence gap; it is NOT automatically "acoustic".

## 2026-10-12 research synthesis (agents G corpus, H verbaliser, I distillation literature)

Reports: `ft/analysis/{G,H,I}` on the box (survival bundle). Labels as in the reports.

- I (literature): teacher labels replacing human labels on the same audio range from a tie (Thorbecke 2024,
  CV-it 17.5 vs 17.5) to ~15 % relative (Hwang 2022, 155M streaming RNNT 5.9 -> 5.1); keeping supervised
  data did not matter with a strong teacher (Hwang Table 5; Thorbecke 17.5 vs 18.4 with +100 h human).
  Filters matter mostly with weak teachers (noisy student Table 4). Soft/logit RNNT KD needs a shared
  vocabulary; across tokenizers only N-best text, Seq-Inter or encoder-feature KD (projection) apply; NeMo has
  no ASR KD trainer (pseudo-labelling only). CAVEAT: the Nemotron card lists MLS, CV and FLEURS in its training
  data: its labels on those train splits (and the 15.75 reference) may be partly memorised.
  Ranked first experiments: (1) additive teacher-labelled audio vs a matched-steps control; (2) label swap on
  the same audio; (3) filter strength. Expected effect sizes: no source supports a range for our setting.
- H (verbaliser): `eou/verbalize_it.py` (self-test 96/96). norm() deletes digits, %, EUR everywhere: digit
  clips were dropped from pseudo-label pools (8.6 %). Eval: 162/865 FLEURS-it refs (18.7 %) contain digits,
  i.e. 1.48 % of FLEURS ref words are deleted on both sides today; val WER definitions stay unchanged; a model
  that learns spoken numbers can pay up to ~1.5 FLEURS insertions (HYPOTHESIS) -> report FLEURS no-digit WER
  as a side metric for label arms.
- G (corpus): `prepare_mix.py` read only `it000` (185 of 1,117 Italian ast shards); Granary-it is ~6.1-6.2 kh
  but its clips are long (mean 17-19.5 s; <= 20 s holds only 26-31 % of hours): 500 h+ needs it100/it101 and
  cutting long clips at silences. Audio is 16 kHz WAV in parquet (~0.113 GB/h); FLAC ~0.53 of PCM16.
  /dev/shm cannot be enlarged (remount denied, OBSERVED): 500 h fits only as FLAC (~35 GB). Teacher ~2.4
  GPU-h for 500 h. Leak: VoxPopuli-it test shares 61/81 speakers and 138/169 session-items with train
  (OBSERVED) -> our VP val numbers are in-session; web clips need an n-gram guard vs the val refs.
  Open: the teacher licence terms for training on its outputs (not checked).

Today's GPU queue (`/root/res/day12.sh`): `scale_8k.sh` (E3-8k, E3G-8k), then `p2_labels.sh` (label swap, E3
recipe, same batches) AS PRE-REGISTERED; no new ablation is inserted before the P2-labels result. NOTE the order

## Experiments, in order

### P0 — Is E3 still undertrained? (optimisation budget)

Two different experiments; never compare them as if they were one:
- (a) fresh 8k/12k cosine with the E3 recipe (`lr_sweep.sh STOP=<n> LRS=2e-4` with `--steps` set to the
  length): its 4k point sits at higher LR than E3@4k; compare end points and slopes.
- (b) continuation from E3@4k (`--init` E3 final.nemo; resets optimizer state and adds a new
  warmup+cosine): a re-warm, interpretable as "more updates after convergence of the first schedule".
Learning curve at 4k / 6k / 8k / 10k / 12k; metrics greedy, beam-8, oracle-8, per-domain WER/CER/empty
(lm_fusion_probe.py at each saved point). Stop early when the frontier is flat over two evals.
Watch memory (E3 peak 21.6 GB at 20 s clips) and disk (1.8 GB per run on the 32 GB root).

### P1 — Additive Granary, not replacement

Keep at least E3's absolute A2 exposure and ADD clean Italian Granary/YODAS exposure. Design the run
in exposure terms (agent F's `diag/exposure_audit.py` replays the sampler exactly):
A2 hours seen (E3: MLS 2.33 passes, CV 0.72, VP 1.16, FLEURS 4.04; 187 h processed in 4000 steps),
Granary hours seen, total processed audio, optimizer steps. Candidate comparison:
E3-long A2-only vs E3-long A2 + additive Granary, matched on A2 exposure (more total steps allowed).
Memory: E3G with 30 s clips peaked 36.5 / 41.9 GB reserved — consider max-dur 20-25 for Granary.

### P2 — Nemotron sequence-level distillation (probably the main lever)

Nemotron so far was teacher/reference/audit/filter. Now: Italian audio -> Nemotron transcript ->
120M student target (hard pseudo-labels; no logit KD at first). Incremental scale, bounded by
storage/cost: 100 h -> 500 h -> 1 kh -> 2 kh+.
Per clip keep: source/dataset, duration, teacher transcript (raw + normalised), original transcript
when one exists, teacher/original agreement WER when available, teacher empty/failure, language-ID,
and the metadata needed to reproduce (shard, offsets, teacher version). Never silently trust the
original transcript: the design must allow original-label vs teacher-label arms.
Existing pieces: `diag/teacher_ws.py` (Nemotron through the CUDA server, ~270x real time: 152 h in
2035 s on 2026-10-10 -> ~1.9 GPU-h for 500 h, ~7.4 GPU-h for 2 kh), `prepare_mix.py --yg-*`,
`pseudo_subsets.py` (agreement subsets, digit drop), `diag/pseudo_stats.py`.
Constraints to solve before 500 h+: 16 kHz int16 is ~115 MB/h -> 500 h ~57 GB > /dev/shm (62 GB) and the
32 GB root disk: store compressed (FLAC) on a larger volume or stream from the source parquet.
Italian availability: espnet/yodas-granary Italian YODAS part ~6.1k h (our inventory 2026-10-10;
the nvidia/Granary card does not state Italian hours). Digits: verbalise numbers instead of dropping
8.6 % of the clips. Filter heuristics from the sources: WER threshold vs a reference label when one
exists (Distil-Whisper), repetition / word-length / words-per-second checks (EMNLP 2024 H1-H3).

### P3 — Distillation beyond hard labels (feasibility note only, after P2)

Questions: can posteriors/logits/alignments be extracted from Nemotron in our CUDA server or NeMo?
RNNT KD between different tokenizers/output spaces (Nemotron vs the 120M SPE) — token-level KD is not
directly applicable; options: word/frame-level alignment KD, or CTC-style frame posteriors over a
shared character space. Storage of soft targets vs hard labels. If too complex, P2 stays the method.

### P4 — Systematic gap decomposition for every important candidate

Report greedy, beam-8, 8-best oracle, teacher, per domain. Read greedy -> beam as search/ranking
opportunity; beam -> oracle as remaining ranking inside the candidate set; oracle -> teacher as a
candidate/model/evidence gap. Do not attribute oracle -> teacher to the encoder alone without proof:
full encoder adaptation was decisive on 2026-10-11, but predictor/joint/output representation can
still contribute.

### P5 — Predictor / language adaptation (after P0-P2)

The n-gram fusion without gain (agent D: a 4-gram over training transcripts, no unseen text) does not
show that language adaptation is useless. Cheap options: predictor/joint adaptation on teacher-clean
Italian transcripts; NGPU-LM shallow fusion / GPU-PB word boosting (both supported for RNNT in NeMo)
with an LM from large unseen Italian text; the homophone/context errors (ha/a, hanno/anno, nei/ne) as
a targeted test set. NeMo hybrid ASR-TTS (text-only domain adaptation, RNNT supported) is a secondary
idea, not a priority.

### P6 — Italian tokenizer stays FROZEN

tok-B2 (56.06 vs 49.08) was under the undertrained B2 recipe and is confounded; it is NOT evidence that
an Italian SPE is intrinsically worse. Re-test only after P0-P2 settle the recipe: stock vs corrected
Italian SPE under the winning recipe. Both defects found on 2026-10-11 are fixed in code (4be5367):
word-internal piece initialisation (dummy prefix) and the apostrophe normaliser gluing "po' confuso".

## References (read 2026-10-11; summaries state only what the sources support)

- Distil-Whisper, Gandhi et al. 2023 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00430 — large-scale pseudo-labelling
  (21.2k h English, greedy teacher labels) + WER-threshold filtering (lambda 10 drops 45 % of the data,
  avg WER 13.4 -> 11.4 at 10k steps); OOD WER improves fast up to ~3.5 kh and stops after ~14 kh.
  Caveats: AED/English, student keeps the teacher's encoder, loss mixes KL (logit KD) with
  pseudo-label cross-entropy.
- Distil-Whisper training code — https://github.com/huggingface/distil-whisper/tree/main/training —
  pseudo-labelling -> student init -> distillation; example `--wer_threshold 20`, lr 1e-4; README: >= 1 kh
  of data and ~50k steps recommended, < 500 h weak.
- Thorbecke, Zuluaga-Gomez et al., Findings of EMNLP 2024 — https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.976/ —
  closest analogue: a 70M streaming Zipformer transducer trained from scratch on Whisper 1-best pseudo-labels
  only (sequence-level KD); Italian is one of 6 CV languages (200 h): 17.5 WER streaming with large-v3
  labels, equal to the supervised baseline; larger teachers help; filters = repetition + max word length;
  no within-language data-scaling study.
- NVIDIA Granary — https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Granary — ~643k h ASR over 25 languages incl.
  Italian (Italian hours not stated), Whisper-large-v3 two-pass labels with LID and hallucination filters;

- NVIDIA EOU fine-tuning — https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/tree/main/examples/asr/asr_eou — the official
  <EOU>/<EOB> recipe (train/eval scripts, EOU latency / early-cutoff / miss metrics).
- NeMo FastConformer RNNT config — https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/main/examples/asr/conf/fastconformer/fast-conformer_transducer_bpe.yaml —
  from-scratch defaults (AdamW 5e-3, 15k warmup, pred 1x640): not fine-tuning values.
- NeMo LM customization — https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/speech/nightly/asr/asr_language_modeling_and_customization.html —
  NGPU-LM and GPU-PB word boosting apply to pure RNNT (greedy and beam).
